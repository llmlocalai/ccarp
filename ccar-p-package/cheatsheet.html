Per-item method

  1. Underline the specific risk or constraint the stem names.
  2. Locate the matching step on the decision chain (overview page §3).
  3. Eliminate options that solve a different risk, that substitute instruction for structure, or that only detect without preventing.
  4. Pick the lightest option that removes the root cause.
